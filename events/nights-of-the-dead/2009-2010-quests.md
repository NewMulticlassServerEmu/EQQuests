# NotD 2009-2010 research: Digging Their Graves, The Hunt for Tattooed Flesh, Terror of Illis Taberish

Gathered 2026-10-05. Read-only (nothing under C:\Git\test was changed). Scope: only the versions whose zones are GoD
or earlier: Digging [1-22] Nektulos / Lake of Ill Omen, [23-32] Emerald Jungle / Firiona Vie, [~45] the Emerald
Jungle option; Terror [6] Nektulos, [16] Lake of Ill Omen, [26] Firiona Vie, [36] Emerald Jungle.

## 0. Read this first: sources, quoting and coordinates

**Allakhazam (Alla)** blocks direct downloads (CloudFront 403 for curl and for PowerShell, retested today). Every
Alla fact below came through WebFetch, which runs the page through a summarizer. Where this file says "quote", the
summarizer was told to copy the text exactly. Paraphrase risk is marked per block:
- **[V]** = near-verbatim. It matches a second copy (local EQQuests, which was scraped from Alla earlier) or it keeps
  Alla's own typos ("vack", "abd", "thr rnd", "simpeltons", "showvel"), which a summarizer would not invent.
- **[P?]** = may be paraphrased or shortened.

**Coordinate order.** Every coordinate below is in **/loc order (Y, X, Z)** unless it says otherwise:
- Alla NPC pages print "spawns at /waypoint A, B, C". EQ's `/waypoint` takes `<y>, <x>, [<z>]`, the same order as
  /loc (RedGuides command doc). Checked against peq: Liskard /waypoint (-1688, -3072, 116) has a peq spawn2 next to it
  at x=-2976, y=-1694, z=102. The missjackie Emerald Jungle locs (Y ~3900, X ~-2550, Z ~-339) sit next to peq EJ
  spawns at x=-2266..-2846, y=3316..3667, z=-334..-339. So the order is confirmed.
- **EQEmu spawn2 needs x = the SECOND number and y = the FIRST.**
- Map-file "P a, b, c" lines (Brewall/Good maps, and the Loping Plains/Steppes/FoS lists in Alla comments) are
  **negated**: spawn2 x = -a, y = -b, z = c. Checked: Vol T`Vke is map P 860.48, -1981.12 and peq nektulos v1 spawn2
  x=-865, y=1976. Glob is map P 700.83, -1737.83 and spawn2 x=-700, y=1743.

**Nektulos zone-version trap.** Live Nektulos is the Depths of Darkhollow rebuild. Alla calls it "Nektulos Forest
3.0". peq has `nektulos` version 0 = "Original classic Nektulos" (expansion -1..9) and version 1 = "DoDH Nektulos"
(min expansion 10). All live Nektulos coordinates below are **version 1 geometry**. A GoD-era server runs version 0,
so those spots have to be re-picked by hand in version 0's north-east. Lake of Ill Omen, Firiona Vie and Emerald
Jungle were never rebuilt, and their peq spawns line up with the live coordinates (checked above).

## 1. Nights of the Dead: Digging Their Graves (Alla quest 4899)

**Info box (Alla) [V]:** Started by Edmund Strangeways, The Plane of Knowledge. Level 1, max 125, Task, repeatable,
not shroudable, solo (1-1). Era tag "Seeds of Destruction" (it ran 2009). Quest items: Edmund's Shovel, Intricately
Tattooed Skin. Related zones: Barren Coast, Field of Scale, Firiona Vie, Goru'kar Mesa, Lake of Ill Omen, Loping
Plains, Nektulos Forest 3.0, The Buried Sea, The Emerald Jungle, The Steppes. **Rewards: none listed** (Alla shows an
empty reward box; EQQuests also ends at an empty "Rewards:").
"This is a shared Halloween task, available only while Halloween events are open... It begins with Edmund Strangeways
in the Plane of Knowledge and has an 18-hour lockout timer." [V]

### 1.5 The dig mechanic
- "In the zones Edmund mentions, you will find piles of brown rocks called "disturbed earth". You cannot /open or
  attack them, but you can use the click effect on your shovel." Using it in the right zone spawns "either a disturbed
  spirit or a tattooed zombie... Only the zombie drops the skin, so you may need to go through multiple spawns." [V]
- The disturbed earth can be targeted ("Target your pile of rocks... click the shovel", warvok 2010) and **shows in
  the Find window** ("The graves were all listed on the find", nerina819, Nektulos at level 13; Oxgoad 2015 "you can
  use the Find command to locate them"). So on live they are targetable, findable, non-attackable spawns.
- warvok 2010 exploit: clicking while running over the pile let the spawn happen without the shovel timer starting.
  Patch 2014-11-19 fixed it: "It is no longer possible to bypass the shovel cooldown timer."
- Shovel recast 16 min 40 s (1000 s). Players report 3-7 or more digs per skin.
- Spawns seen:
  - **tattooed zombie**: drops Intricately Tattooed Skin. In Nektulos at level 13: "The mummy's were dark blue and it
    took 5 try's with a 15 min wait in between" (nerina819), so it is a mummy model there.
  - **a disturbed spirit** (the page's PH name), **"a tossing spirit"** (Oxgoad 2012), **"a turbulent specter" /
    "turbulent bones"** (2012, "The specter dropped a lucidem and a versluierd fungus"; lucidem is a SoD-era drop, so
    this is probably a high-level zone). **"restless bones"** in Lake of Ill Omen, which are iksar skeletons ("These
    pop up when you dig up 'disturbed earth' for the Digging the Graves Halloween quest", Oxgoad 2015-10-31 on the Alla
    LoIO zone page).
  - None of these spawns has an Alla bestiary page (searches for "tattooed zombie", "a tattooed zombie" and
    "disturbed earth" return nothing; "disturbed spirit" returns only an unrelated level-105 Crypt of Sul mob).
    **Levels, HP, abilities and loot tables for the in-era dig spawns were NOT found.**
- **Dig-spot coordinates:** Alla comments give them only for Loping Plains (Quallain 2018, 26 map-file lines, in
  EQQuests:15651-15676), The Steppes (goldbishop 2011, "P 53.7550, 1682.8251, 10.4556, 127, 127, 0, 1,
  disturbed_earth_(Holloween)" + 13 more) and Field of Scale (Ucnia 2012). All three are out of era. **No coordinates
  were found for Nektulos, Lake of Ill Omen, Emerald Jungle or Firiona Vie** (Alla, Bonzz, web search, and the map
  files in every local client: TSC Client, TSC Client MASTER, the live client, RoF2, THJ).

## 2. Nights of the Dead: The Hunt for Tattooed Flesh (Alla quest 4900)

**Info box (Alla) [V]:** Level 1, max 125, Task, repeatable, not shroudable, solo 1-1, era Seeds of Destruction.
Quest items: Edmund's Shovel (85631), Intricately Tattooed Skin (85633). Related creatures: Edmund Strangeways
(npc=33988), a frightening liaison (npc=33989). Zone: Plane of Knowledge.
"This task is automatically assigned to you when you complete the task 'Digging Their Graves'... for the first time.
The first step should automatically update. You now need to do the 'Digging Their Graves' task four (4) more times"
(Bonzz). It is a grand task: the 5 Digging turn-ins drive it.

### 2.1 Task steps [V]
Five steps, all the same text, all in the Plane of Knowledge:
```
Edmund has inspected the tattooed skin you delivered 0/1 (Plane of Knowledge)
(x5)
```
**"The Tattooed Flesh inspections in PoK" are these five steps:** Edmund "inspects" the skin you hand in on each
Digging turn-in. There is **no** separate object or NPC to inspect anywhere in PoK. Bonzz: "make sure you hail and /
or follow along with Edmund Strangeways, in order to get the proper updates for this task... Each time there will be
a different, but continued, conversation."

### 2.2 Task description and completion text [V]
- Description: "Edmund Strangeways' hands tremble as he holds up the tattooed skin. 'I've seen these markings before,
  once on a corpse in the mass graves of a plague-ridden village, again in a forgotten crypt in a dark forest half a
  continent away. But they were like two pieces of the same puzzle. Both times it was during Harvester's Haunt, and
  I've been searching ever since. Can you find more?'"
- Completion: "You have assisted Edmund in completing a map tattooed on the skin of long dead worshipers of
  Bertoxxulous." then "You have been given: Frightening Writ".
