# Nights of the Dead 2006-2007: Skeleton Zapping, Troublemakers in Faydark, Undead Rising

Gathered 2026-10-05. This was read-only research and nothing under C:\Git\test was touched. Scratch downloads (screenshots,
the PEQ quest clone, map clones, `pq.py`) are in `scratchpad\notd_web\`.

## How to read this file

- **How the quotes were collected.** Allakhazam blocks raw downloads (curl and PowerShell both return 403), so every
  Allakhazam text here came through WebFetch. WebFetch passes the page through a small model. When I asked for verbatim
  text it returned full `You say, '...'` / `NPC says '...'` transcripts. Those transcripts are **probably verbatim but
  not guaranteed**. Each block below is marked with its confidence:
  - **[V+]**: the same wording came back from two or more separate sources or comments (strong).
  - **[V]**: one verbatim-style transcript from one fetch (probably exact; check punctuation in game if it matters).
  - **[P]**: a paraphrase or summary from the fetch model. Do not use it as NPC text.
- **Coordinates.** Allakhazam's "+A, +B" NPC locations are in **/loc order (Y, X)**. I proved this two ways: the PEQ
  spawn2 row for Ordun the Bonecollector is x=205, y=-138, and Allakhazam lists him at "-137, +205". The EQ map files
  (Brewall/Good, `P` lines store -X, -Y) also match Allakhazam for Silas, Gravlin and Oxrun. Bonzz's "/waypoint" values
  use the same order.
- **Item ids.** Allakhazam item ids (69371 and so on) are Allakhazam-internal. The **live ids** come from the local PEQ
  dump (`items.source='13THFLOOR'`, the live-data import). EQResource item and achievement pages returned 403, so I
  could not cross-check the ids there. Allakhazam's stat blocks match the PEQ rows on every flag I compared.
- **EQEmu flag semantics** in the PEQ rows below: `nodrop=0` means NO TRADE, `norent=0` means TEMPORARY,
  `loregroup=-1` means LORE, and `questitemflag=1` means QUEST ITEM.

## 1. Nights of the Dead: Skeleton Zapping (Allakhazam quest 3803), 2006

### Header (Allakhazam, [V])
- Level 10, Min 10, Max 125. Drewinette, 2014: "it can be done at level 100, therefore 'Maximum level: 90' should be
  adjusted", so the max was 90 originally.
- Solo, min 1 / max 1 players. Era tag: Serpent's Spine. Quest added Oct 31, 2006.
- Header says "Repeatable: No" but also "Failure Lockout 3 hours, Success Lockout 3 hours". The comments settle it:
  - Nov 3, 2007: "3 hour timer on quest."
  - Drewinette, Oct 18, 2014 [V]: "This task is repeatable. When completing it : 'You have received a replay timer for
    'Skeleton Zapping' 0d:3h:0m remaining.'"
  - Oxgoad, 2020: refused after 3 hours. Dogpaw explains that the refusal can come from having already done it (the
    Monster Mash achievement).
  - **Conclusion: a repeatable solo task with a 3-hour replay timer.**
- Refusal text (bschenk 2012 and Tavington 2019, [V+]): `Sorry <name>, I don't have anything for someone with your
  abilities.` This is the standard task-ineligible message.

### Dialogue (Uzek the Bonecollector transcript [V]; the opening line is [V+], also quoted on the Filada, Kordulaf and Ordun pages)
```
You say, 'Hail, Uzek the Bonecollector'
Uzek the Bonecollector says 'Greetings, ____. You look familiar, but now is not the time for pleasantries. A skeleton uprising has began to overwhelm this area. If we don't [act fast] we will be out of a home in no time at all.'
You say, 'Act fast?'
Uzek the Bonecollector says 'If you are willing to [lend a hand] we just might be able to expel the wretches back to their graves.'
You say, 'I will lend a hand'
Uzek the Bonecollector says 'Excellent. Your efforts will not go unnoticed.'
You have been assigned the task 'Skeleton Zapping'.
```
- "has began" is live's typo; keep it.
- Every Bonecollector uses the same script with their own name in it. This is confirmed by fragments on the Filada,
  Kordulaf, Ordun, Renla, Rentila and Khbantiz pages.
- The scythe is handed out at assignment. Live gives no separate "You receive" line in the transcript. The item comes
  with the task (Allakhazam's item page: obtained through the quest).

### Task
- Description [V]: *A recent uprising must be dealt with! Use the Scythe of Skeletal Expulsion to destroy as many of them as you can.*
- Step [V]: `Expel 10 skeletons with the Scythe of Skeletal Expulsion 0/10 (ALL)` (any zone).
- Each update comes from the scythe's effect killing a qualifying skeleton. The reward is granted **automatically on
  the 10th kill**: "Reward (received after you kill the last one)". There is no return to the NPC.
- Reward: **5x Ginormous Jawbreaker**. XP: nerina819 (2019) "level 7 ... Received over 30% experience". The exact XP
  value is unknown.
- You do not have to zap in the zone where you got the task (comment 2014: got it in Crescent Reach, zapped in
  Freeport).

## 2. Nights of the Dead: Troublemakers in Faydark (Allakhazam quest 4318), 2007

The Allakhazam page says "Era: The Buried Sea (October 2007)". The Silas NPC page was added 2007-11-02 and the first
comments date from Nov 3, 2007.

### Header (Allakhazam)
Min 1, Max 90, repeatable, no time limit, solo (min 1 / max 1). **Lockout: none stated.**

### NPC: Silas Lightweaver (Allakhazam npc 26704)
- Greater Faydark at **+490, +395, 0** (/loc Y, X, Z), "in the forest below Kelethin"; quest page: "490, 395 (southwest
  of the orc lift)". The Good's map file has `P -397.05, -490.79, -3.65`, which gives /loc **490.8, 397.1, -3.7**.
  Bonzz's "/waypoint 495, -400" has the wrong sign on X; the map file and Allakhazam agree.
- Level 85 on Allakhazam (an 83 cleric saw him con yellow).
- Look from the screenshot: **elf male (Wood Elf or High Elf), blond, tan/brown leather armor**, unarmed. Suffix
  "(Nights of the Dead Quests)"; the light-show shot shows "(Halloween)".
- Screenshots: https://zam.zamimg.com/images/e/7/e7a629c0cc89b5f2004d2d5ba3c6430a.jpg (thumb
  https://zam.zamimg.com/images/d/7/d74a65770ff0ff36daa1e24b7adcfe51.th96.png). Light show:
  https://everquest.allakhazam.com/mediabox/halloween-faydark.jpg (Kelethin platforms filled with firework particles).

### Dialogue (Allakhazam walkthrough [V]; the shorter fragments on the NPC page and the first fetch match it)
```

You say, 'I am interested in helping'
```

Completion [V]:
```
You say, 'Hail, Silas Lightweaver'
Silas Lightweaver says 'You've done well _____, a decided calm has descended upon this shady grove once again... The perfect atmosphere for a display of lights and color. Here, take these fireworks and join in with the display; just give me a few moments, and I will begin.'
```
- The Allakhazam page itself mixes "faeries" and "fairies"; keep both as written.
- The page's first hail line reads "You say, 'Silas Lightweaver'" (the word Hail is missing). I normalised it to
  'Hail, Silas Lightweaver'.
- The fetch showed a stray `'"Yes,` (an extra double quote, an Allakhazam typo). I dropped it.
- The page did not give the exact order of the "assigned" and "receive" lines; the order above is mine.

### Task steps [V]
1. `Capture pixie and faerie troublemakers in Greater Faydark 0/10 (Greater Faydark)`. Click the bottle on the
   targeted fairy. "The bottle must be used within melee range of the pixie or faerie targeted." The 10-second cast
   can be ruined by damage shields or ripostes killing the target. The advice: remove damage shields, face away, pull
   with stones or non-damaging spells.
2. `Release the troublemakers back into Lesser Faydark 0/1 (Lesser Faydark)`. Right-click the bottle anywhere in
   Lesser Faydark.
3. `Return to Silas Lightweaver, and help perform a light show 0/1 (Greater Faydark)`. Hail him. Reward: **10x
   Firework: Nagafen Fire**. The show starts after a short delay ("just give me a few moments"). The fetch summary says
   "approximately one minute" [P]. Players need `/showspelleffects ON` to see it [V].

### Mobs
- **a faerie troublemaker** (Allakhazam 26705): level 1, Greater Faydark, "No factions hits". Loot listed: Cat's Eye
  Agate, Pixie Dust, Pixie Wing. Look: classic **fairy** model, female, pink dress, blond, white wings. Screenshot
  https://zam.zamimg.com/images/a/3/a3d90a38cc7ed56ad40ca13349fbad89.png
- **a pixie troublemaker** (Allakhazam 26706): level 1, Greater Faydark. Look: blue-skinned **pixie** model with
  iridescent wings. Screenshot https://zam.zamimg.com/images/d/0/d00ac859de3dd557ccd582a267ee156a.png (uploaded
  Nov 2, 2007 by Railus).
- Where they spawn [P]: "found commonly around a large radius near the orc camps south of Crushbone". Also [P]:
  "immune to run speed changes".
- Counting rule [V+]:
  - Savingyer, Nov 6, 2007: "Any Fairies you catch will count towards your total. Not just the troublemakers."
  - Wreck, 2015: "Regular Fairies give a faction hit when you kill them. The troublemakers don't."
  - Diepen, 2022: "But you don't have to kill them, just bottle them... No faction hit."
  - So bottling is a capture (the target is removed), not a kill, and any GFay fairy or pixie counts.
- Spawn counts, respawn timers and spawn points are **not published**.

## 3. Nights of the Dead: Undead Rising (Allakhazam quest 5068), 2007

### The year
The Allakhazam page says "Era: Seeds of Destruction (October 2009)" because the quest entry was only *added* on
May 2, 2010. The quest is from **2007**:
- Corporal Gravlin's NPC page was added 2007-11-05.
- The Firestorm Torch item comments are dated Oct 30 and Oct 31, 2007 (FCseven: "This is the reward for finishing the
  task Undead Rising").
- The Allakhazam wiki and Bonzz both list it under 2007.

### Header (Allakhazam [V])
Min 15, Max 90, repeatable, solo (min 1 / max 1). Time limit and lockout are **not stated**. The Torch of Undead
Warding page shows "Required Level: 15" (PEQ has reqlevel 0).

### NPC: Corporal Gravlin (Allakhazam npc 26718)
- Qeynos Hills, Allakhazam "-74, +59 (near North Qeynos zoneline)". Comments give "-66, +60" (asusazazel) and
  "-61, 59, -5" (phelixx). Good's map file has `P -59.06, 73.95, -7.53`, which gives /loc **-73.9, 59.1, -7.5**.
- In PEQ terms that is about 275 units north of the qeytoqrg-to-qeynos2 zone line (y = -350). The Surefall zone line
  is at qeytoqrg y = 5200, x = 91, so the route runs north along x ≈ 60-100.
- Level 60. Look from the screenshot: **Human male in Qeynos guard uniform**: gold full-face helm, gold tabard with the
  Qeynos crest, dark chain sleeves and legs, gold bracers. Suffix "(Nights of the Dead Quests)".
- Screenshot https://zam.zamimg.com/images/a/e/ae075814526ac1611ea91cb2cfe4b7b0.jpg (thumb
  https://zam.zamimg.com/images/2/2/22139a9d143a757a9993a119df39efc5.th96.png).

- Players typed "dangerous", "others", "interested" and "ready", so the keywords are dangerous / others / interested /
  ready.
- "there the journey is currently" is live's wording.
- The Amio transcript puts "You have been assigned..." and "Receive Torch" before the hand-over emote; the walkthrough
  puts them after. The order is not certain.

### Task steps [V+]
1. `Let corporal Gravlin know when you are [ready] 0/1 (ALL)`. Say "ready".
2. `Escort the traveler safely to Surefall Glade 0/1 (ALL)`. Updates "Once you reach Surefall". It is unknown whether
   the trigger is the traveler reaching the zone line or you entering qrg.
3. `Report back to Corporal Gravlin 0/1 (ALL)`. Hail him. Reward: **Firestorm Torch**.

### Escort mechanics (walkthrough and comments)
- **Traveler spawn.** "A frightened traveler spawns as soon as you get the task. It begins to walk when you say
  'ready' to Gravlin." [V]
- **Multiple players.** Six players who each have the task and say ready at the same time spawn six travelers
  (Synicasm, 2018).
- **Route.** "takes a direct route to Surefall Glade" [P]. Alowyn saw it end by walking into the wall about 30 feet
  east of the tunnel entrance.
- **Waves.**
  - "When it passes the split in road where the stone marker is, 2x 'minion of Pyzjn' spawn" [V]. Amio: "two 'minion
    of Pyzjn' spawn at a time".
  - "As you get closer to Surefall Glade, undead will spawn and attack the traveler" (asusazazel).
  - Ironic (2017) [V]: "groups of undead popping in pretty rapid succession (~5 seconds). These are at around the
    halfway point and the 3/4 point." Advice: "wait until at least the third group of undead has popped before
    clicking the torch."
  - Alowyn: "at least 6 (and frequently more) adds on the traveler".
  - Synicasm: "Sometimes only 3 or so spawn, wait a few seconds and more will spawn".
- **The minions keep hurting the traveler from range.** Ironic: "they are able to do damage to the traveler no matter
  how far away they are". Alowyn: "they're doing damage to the traveler while they [are] running toward him".
- **Crowd control.**
  - Mez and root work, but then the minions are out of torch range and still deal damage (Ironic).
  - AE fear, AE taunt, AE slow and debuffs work (Synicasm).
  - Ranger Vinelash on the traveler pulls aggro off it and does not slow it (2015 comment).
  - Pre-69 enchanter PBAE stuns work (Alowyn).
- **The traveler cannot be healed or buffed** (Wreck 2015; phelixx 2008: "Buffs and heals do not appear to work on
  the NPC traveler"). One 2010 commenter said you cannot slow the traveler either.
- **Killing skeletons without the torch.** Wreck: "The skellies can be killed by nuking or dots". The 2010
  commenter: "the skeletons cannot be killed without the torch". These conflict.
- **Failure.** "If the traveler dies, the quest ends and you will have to get it again" (asusazazel). When the
  task fails, the torch disappears: Wreck, "When the Alt's Traveler died, her torch poofed".
- **Timer.** No escort time limit was reported.
- **Difficulty.** It is hard: "took 7 attempts" (Alowyn); a success "with 4% health" (Wreck). The standard tactic is
  several characters with torches taking turns.
