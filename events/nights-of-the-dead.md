# Nights of the Dead: Bone Mask of Horror

**Quest Type:** Quest | **Repeatable:** once per year (Halloween season) | **Allakhazam:** quest=5371

Starts with **Zigan Ribshard** near the Cabilis stone in the Plane of Knowledge (/way -639.95, 179.29, -156).

Zigan Ribshard says 'Another sad little adventurer looking for a new conquest eh? Well I've got something for you but
you'll need to check in with a few [friends] of mine before you can have anything to do with it.'

He points you to five people in Kithicor Forest. Complete all five:

1. Trick or Treat for the Old Man - Old Man Draykey (solo quest, Allakhazam quest=3254)
2. The Hungry Halfling - Mippie Diggs (solo task, quest=4665)
3. Missing Costume Pieces - a Dressed-Up halfling (shared task, quest=4898)
4. The Bone Collector - Barsin, the Bone Collector (group task, quest=5369)
5. Out With the Old - a silly puppet (group monster mission, quest=5370; needs a Bristlebane's Ticket of Admission,
   which each of the first four awards)

Return to Zigan **while wearing an illusion costume**. Without one:
Zigan Ribshard says 'You still look like yourself though. Didn't you hear me when I said to bring a costume? Now get out
of here and don't come back until you have a good costume!'

In costume:
Zigan Ribshard says 'This mask is one of my finer creations. Use it well and go scare some rotten elf kid.'

Reward by level (live item ids):

| Level | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| under 10 | Dusty Bone Mask of Horror (45520) |
| 10-20 | Cracked Bone Mask of Horror (45519) |
| 20-30 | Hardened Bone Mask of Horror (45521) |
| 30-40 | Strengthened Bone Mask of Horror (45523) |
| 40-50 | Calcified Bone Mask of Horror (45518) |
| 50-60 | Solid Bone Mask of Horror (45522) |
| over 60 | Bone Mask of Horror (45517) |

Bone Mask of Horror (top tier): FACE, AC 15, STR 15, DEX 5, STA 10, WIS 15, INT 15, AGI 5, HP/Mana/End 135, saves Fire 5,
Disease 10, Cold 5, Magic 15, Poison 5. Effect: Illusion: Frost Bone. Lore, No Trade.
Notes from players: a destroyed mask is replaced by Zigan if you return in illusion.

# Nights of the Dead: The Hungry Halfling

**Level:** 10-125 | **Quest Type:** Task, solo | **Repeatable:** Yes | **Allakhazam:** quest=4665

Starts with **Mippie Diggs** in Kithicor Forest (about 1950, 3800, near the Rivervale zone line).

You say, 'Hail, Mippie Diggs'
Mippie Diggs says 'Hail to you too. You wouldn't happen to have any of those wondrous [pumpkiny foods] on you would you?
I'm not really feel my best and nothing perks you up better than a nice bit of tasty pumpkin.'
You say, 'Pumpkiny foods?'
Mippie Diggs says 'The best of the best pumpkiny foods can be found in my personal book of home remedies. They may be a
bit complicated to make, but you've never had anything tastier. I'd be happy to give you a copy of my book if you'd be
[willing] to bring me the first couple of snacks you make.'
You say, 'I am willing.'
Mippie Diggs says 'Thank you so much. Here is the book, now see what sort of things you can come up with.'
You receive Mippie's Home Remedies. Mippie Diggs says 'Remember bring me the first snacks you make from that book!'

Make any five foods from the book and hand them to Mippie ("My, my, this looks good! Thank you ...!").

Recipes in Mippie's Home Remedies:
- Pumpkin Pie (Baking, trivial 68): 2 cups of flour, 2 bits of pumpkin flesh, 2 bits of egg batter, bottle of milk,
  packet of pumpkin spice, cup of sugar, pie tin
- Pumpkin Bread (Baking, trivial 102): 4 cups of flour, bit of pumpkin flesh, yeast, 2 bits of egg batter, bread tin
- Spiced Pumpkin Cider (Brewing, trivial 68): 2 bits of pumpkin flesh, barley, hops, pumpkin cider spice, yeast, bottle
- Pumpkin Shake (Baking, trivial 41): bit of pumpkin flesh, bottle of sweet milk, Ice of Velious, rock salt
- Baked Pumpkin Seeds: 2 handfuls of pumpkin seeds, jar of butter, sprinkle of ground pepper, copper sheet

Rewards: Bristlebane's Ticket of Admission, plus one random 5-dose race illusion potion (Essence of Barbarian, Dark Elf,
Drakkin, Dwarf, Erudite, Gnome, Gukta, Half Elf, Halfling, High Elf, Human, Iksar, Ogre, Troll, Vah Shir or Wood Elf).
Notes from players: any five foods in any mix count; pumpkin flesh drops from the scarecrows outside the mansion in Unrest.

# Nights of the Dead: Out With the Old

**Level:** 20-125 | **Quest Type:** Group monster mission (3-6) | **Time limit / lockout:** 6 hours | **Allakhazam:** quest=5370

Starts with **a silly puppet** outside a cabin in north-central Kithicor Forest (about 1230, 1060). Needs a Bristlebane's
Ticket of Admission (from Trick or Treat for the Old Man, The Hungry Halfling, Missing Costume Pieces or The Bone Collector).

You say, 'Hail, a silly puppet'
a silly puppet says 'Whadda ya want? Can't ya see I'm busy here? Do you think that I just 'hang' around here all day?
Hahah, get it? I'm on strings! Bah, you bore me. Go bug the dummy here before I get mad.'
You say, 'Hail, Cathil'
Cathil says 'Why hello there! It seems that we are not alone in our curiosity of these woods. Let me introduce myself, I am
Cathil and this here is Wenan...' (the talk leads to wandering story tellers and a tale)
Give the puppet the ticket: a silly puppet says 'Oh...ok then...I got a story for you alright but don't think of this as
a history lesson...' - you are assigned Out With the Old.

Steps: Fight for control of reality (Kithicor Forest); claim your reward.
Players take one of six god templates: Terris Thule (enchanter), Tunare (druid), Cazic Thule (shadow knight), Rallos Zek
(warrior), Erollisi Marr (cleric), Bertoxxulous (necromancer).
Waves: (1) a fragment of spite + 16 lost creatures of the void; (2) a shard of hate + 12 figments of malice; (3) a shard of
nothingness + 9 creatures of despair, then a sliver of despondency + 9 fragments of malice, then The Legion Commander of
the Void + 9 wandering souls of the void. Killing the Legion Commander wins; loot the shield from the chest.
Rewards by level: Chipped (63750), Cracked (63751), Fractured (63752), Lesser (46174), Splintered (58571), Split (63754)
Shield of the Void, Shield of the Void (46176), Shield of the Void [artifact] (46195); Spell: Illusion: Frost Bone (61079).

# Nights of the Dead: The Bone Collector - extra details (Allakhazam quest=5369)

Full dialogue and steps: `expansions/01-ruins-of-kunark.md`. Barsin stands at -110, 25 in Kithicor Forest (north edge of
the lake in the south-east). Group task, 3-6 players, success lockout 6 hours. After the delivery Barsin examines the
bones; a bone construct spawns and attacks, and killing it completes the task.

| Version | Bones | Possible rewards (plus Bristlebane's Ticket of Admission 45441 and experience) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 20 | 4 Bone Chips (Befallen), 2 Warbone Chips (Estate of Unrest), 4 Bone Chips (Kurn's Tower) | Carved Bone Totem |
| 41 | 4 Bone Chips (Tower of Frozen Shadow), 2 bone chips from plaguebone skeletons (any zone), 2 Iced Bone Chips (Lower Guk) | Engraved Bone Earring |
| 60 | Ghast Fingerbones (ire ghasts, Plane of Hate), 4 Bone Chips (crypt skeletons, Old Sebilis), a dragon rib (Dragon Necropolis) | Bone-Studded Cloak, Carved Bone Totem, Construct's Blackened Bone Earring (61394), Glowing Skull Pendant |
| 69+ | Splintered Discordling Bone (rare, Wall of Slaughter), 4 Bone Chips (Plane of Nightmare), dragon skull fragments (ground spawn, Dragon Necropolis tunnels about 1819, 148, -258) | Construct's Blackened Bone Earring |

How the reward works (Allakhazam comments): the "possible rewards" are the bone construct's LOOT, one of the version's
items ("a bone construct dropped: Carved Bone Totem", "...: Engraved Bone Earring", "...: Construct's Blackened Bone
Earring"). Barsin gives only the experience and the ticket. Level 10+, repeatable, "Success Lockout Timer: 06:00:00".
The version follows the group's average level; live never published where one version ends and the next starts.

What the NPCs look like (researched 2026-10-05; Allakhazam screenshots matched to PEQ rows of the same model):
- **Barsin, the Bone Collector**: a scarecrow (race 82, neuter) - jack-o'-lantern head, blue coat-robe with a red sash,
  ragged legs; level 50. "he's a scarecrow standing by the lake at loc -101, +9" (Allakhazam npc 31716). Confirmed.
- **a bone construct**: a skeletal dragon. "a skeletal dragon mob scaled to the level of the group with an ability to FD
  you... The mob summons" (Allakhazam item 61079); "It is undead (hit for 1K, will feign death you, it is hasted and it
  cannot be rooted/snared)" (bonzz.com, 69+ version, level 71-72). Race 122 (dragon skeleton) is a likely match, not
  confirmed by a screenshot. The level-20 version "blue con to lvl 30".

# Nights of the Dead: Missing Costume Pieces - extra details (Allakhazam quest=4898)

Full dialogue and steps: `expansions/03-shadows-of-luclin.md`. The items are the Bloody Vampire Cloak and the Bloody
Vampire Fangs, looted from the Bloody Vampire and returned to the Dressed-Up halfling (1090, -570 in Kithicor Forest).
- 53 version: the vampire roams Katta Castellum between the Mausoleum (-700, -1532, 12) and Tsaph Katta Hall (200, 80, 69).
- 58 version: north-west of The Maiden's Eye near the Dawnshroud Peaks entrance, with about 12 infernal bats.
- 66+ version: Plane of Hate (old), the room with Innoruuk, with 12-15 bats (hits up to about 235); all mezzable.

Rewards (each also gives a Bristlebane Ticket of Admission):

| Version | Rewards |
| --- | --- |
| 30 | Band of Mortality (61547), Harrowing Black Silk Cape (65016) |
| 40 | Eerie Silver Band (60355), Spooky Stone Totem (65081) |
| 53 | Earring of Spectral Essence (63450), Ghoulish Bolster Belt (62176) |
| 58 | Blood Talon Necklace (60678), Earring of Ghostly Chants (62090) |
| 66+ | Bloody Vampiric Cloak (61381), Ring of Haunted Visions (46169) |

The task window lists both items, but players report getting ONE of them (one got the Harrowing Black Silk Cape from the
30 version, another the Ring of Haunted Visions from 66+). Level 20+, repeatable, no lockout listed, 1-6 players.
Task steps: search for the vampire; recover the cloak; recover the fangs (loot); return the cloak; return the fangs.

The vampire and his bats (researched 2026-10-05):
- In game he is **"Bloody Vampire"** (log: "Bloody Vampire scowls at you... (Lvl: 45)"); the quest text says Blood Vampire.
- Levels seen per version: Castle Mistmoore 35, Tenebrous Mountains 45, Katta Castellum 55, The Maiden's Eye 60, Plane of
  Hate 66 (Allakhazam npc 38043 / 45406 / 41105 / 45405 / 19724). Castle Mistmoore: he roams from the castle entrance to
  the northern valley.
- Look: the LDoN vampire (race 360, male, texture 1) - black hooded robe with red lining, purple tunic, pale face
  (same model as Darion the Blade). Likely, from screenshots.
- Bats: "an infernal bat" (npc 19725; level 61 in the Plane of Hate). Counts reported: Maiden's Eye about 12, Plane of
  Hate 12-15 (older posts: 10), Tenebrous "they spawn 5 at a time", Castle Mistmoore 3 ("3 adds MM"). Race probably 34
  (classic giant bat, angular wings); the one screenshot is dark.

**a Dressed-Up halfling** looks like a vampire, not a halfling: the LDoN undead-vampire model (race 359, male) -
pale-lavender skin, pointed ears, long white hair, green ragged tunic (same model as a Bloodguard aspirant; Allakhazam
npc 23816, level 75). Likely.

**Zigan Ribshard** is a goblin (race 433; red skin, loincloth), level 50, male in the 2005 patch notes ("he"). PEQ has
him as gender 1, which the RoF2 client draws as a human; the client's goblin 433 model is gender 2 only.
